# China Food Delivery for Foreigners (2026): Meituan and Ele.me Complete Guide

Food delivery in China is a way of life. With over 500 million users, it's fast, cheap, and incredibly diverse. As a foreigner, you can order anything from street food to Michelin-starred restaurant meals, delivered to your door in 20-40 minutes for a delivery fee of ¥3-8.

The challenge isn't the technology — it's navigating a Chinese-language interface, getting your address right, and handling the few moments where things go wrong.

## Platform Overview

### Meituan (美团) — Market Leader

Meituan is the dominant delivery platform with the widest restaurant coverage. It's accessible as a standalone app or as a mini-program within Alipay.

**Why use Meituan:**
- Largest restaurant selection
- Alipay mini-program (no separate download needed)
- Photo menus and user ratings
- Integrated with hotel bookings and local services

### Ele.me (饿了么) — Strong Alternative

Ele.me (owned by Alibaba) is the #2 platform. In many areas, coverage is comparable to Meituan.

**Why use Ele.me:**
- Sometimes has exclusive restaurants
- Often runs aggressive promotions
- WeChat mini-program available
- Slightly better English support in some areas

**Recommendation:** Install both. Compare delivery fees and availability for your specific address. Use whichever has better options in your neighborhood.

## Getting Started: Setup

### Step 1: Access the Platform

**Meituan:**
1. Open Alipay → search "美团外卖" → access mini-program
2. Or download the standalone Meituan app (美团)
3. Log in with Alipay account

**Ele.me:**
1. Open Alipay → search "饿了么" → access mini-program
2. Or open WeChat → search "饿了么" → access mini-program
3. Log in with your Alipay/WeChat account

### Step 2: Set Your Delivery Address

This is where most foreigners fail. Your address must be precise enough for a rider to find you among thousands of apartments.

**What you need:**
- Your building name in Chinese (e.g., 朝阳大悦城公寓)
- Building number and gate (e.g., 3号楼 2单元)
- Floor and room number (e.g., 1502室)
- A landmark nearby (e.g., 大悦城东门对面)

**How to get it:**
- Ask your hotel reception to write your complete address in Chinese
- Copy the address from your booking confirmation
- Take a photo of any address card your hotel provides

**Pro tip:** Save your address in your phone's clipboard so you can paste it every time.

### Step 3: Confirm Payment

Both platforms pay via Alipay or WeChat Pay. Ensure your [payment setup](/insights/alipay-vs-wechat-pay-foreigner) is working before your first order. Make a small test purchase to verify.

## How to Order: Step-by-Step

### 1. Browse Restaurants

- Filter by distance (nearest first)
- Sort by rating (4.5+ is reliable)
- Look for restaurants with many reviews and photo menus
- Monthly sales count (月售) indicates popularity — 1000+ means proven quality

### 2. Choose Your Food

- **Photo menus** — Most restaurants include photos of each dish
- **Ratings and comments** — Check user photos and comments below each item
- **Spice level** — Look for 辣 (spicy), 微辣 (mild spicy), or 不辣 (not spicy)
- **Portion size** — Comments often mention portion sizes

### 3. Customize Your Order

- **口味 (taste/flavor):** 少盐 (less salt), 少油 (less oil), 不要香菜 (no cilantro)
- **数量 (quantity):** Adjust portions
- **备注 (notes):** Add special instructions in Chinese if needed

### 4. Add Delivery Notes

In the delivery notes field (配送备注), add:
- Your phone number (in case the rider can't reach you via app)
- Gate or entrance instructions (e.g., "从东门进，3号楼")
- Any delivery time preferences

### 5. Track and Receive

- Watch the rider's real-time location on the map
- The rider will call or message when they arrive
- Most deliveries are left at the building entrance or delivered to your door
- Rate your order afterward (this helps the restaurant and rider)

## Common Pain Points and Solutions

### Problem: Rider Can't Find You

**Solution:**
- Pin your exact location on the map before ordering
- Add landmark-based notes: "大门口左边，红色门旁边"
- Keep your phone volume up and answer calls
- Ask hotel reception to write pickup instructions in Chinese

### Problem: Payment Fails

**Solution:**
- Switch between Alipay and WeChat Pay
- Check that your payment verification is complete
- Ensure your linked card hasn't expired
- See our [Alipay](/insights/alipay-vs-wechat-pay-foreigner) and [WeChat Pay](/insights/wechat-pay-foreigner) guides

### Problem: Wrong Order Delivered

**Solution:**
- Screenshot your order confirmation before delivery
- Use the in-app complaint/refund function (申请退款)
- Take a photo of what you received vs. what you ordered
- Most platforms process refunds within 24 hours

### Problem: Menu Is All in Chinese

**Solution:**
- Use photo menus to visually choose dishes
- Take a screenshot and use [Pleco or Google Translate](/insights/best-translation-apps-china-travel) camera OCR
- Learn basic food vocabulary (see below)

## Tips for Better Orders

1. **Order during off-peak hours** (before 11:30 AM or after 1:30 PM for lunch) for faster delivery.
2. **Check the rain.** During bad weather, delivery times double and some restaurants close.
3. **Try local favorites.** Every city has specialties — ask locals or check top-rated local restaurants.
4. **Use the "recommend" feature.** Both platforms suggest dishes based on your area and popularity.
5. **Save favorite restaurants.** Once you find a reliable spot, bookmark it for easy reordering.
6. **Read recent reviews.** Check reviews from the past week for current quality assessment.

## Alternatives to Delivery Apps

If you can't get the apps working:

- **Hotel room service:** Most hotels offer delivery menus with English
- **WeChat groups:** Many expat neighborhoods have food delivery WeChat groups
- **Restaurant direct:** Find a nearby restaurant on [Amap](/insights/google-maps-in-china-alternatives) and call directly
- **Front desk help:** Ask hotel staff to order on your behalf
