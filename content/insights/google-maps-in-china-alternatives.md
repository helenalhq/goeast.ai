# Does Google Maps Work in China? Best Navigation Alternatives (2026)

One of the first things travelers discover in China is that Google Maps is essentially broken. Map tiles load slowly, place names are outdated, and real-time navigation simply doesn't work.

This isn't a temporary glitch — Google's mapping data for China has been degraded since the company's services were restricted. Here's what to use instead.

## Why Google Maps Fails in China

Google Maps relies on real-time data from Google's servers, which are blocked in mainland China. Even with a VPN, the underlying map data for China is outdated and incomplete:

- **No real-time traffic:** Traffic conditions, road closures, and construction updates are missing
- **Outdated POI data:** Restaurants, shops, and landmarks may be wrong or missing entirely
- **No transit navigation:** Bus routes, subway schedules, and transfer information are unreliable
- **GPS drift:** China uses a different coordinate system (GCJ-02), which can cause a 100-600m offset if your app doesn't account for it

## The Best Alternatives

### 1. Apple Maps (iPhone Users) — Easiest Option

If you use an iPhone, Apple Maps is your simplest solution. Apple licenses Chinese map data from Amap (Gaode), so you get accurate local data with a familiar English interface.

**Strengths:**
- English place names in all major cities
- Accurate driving and walking directions
- Subway and bus transit directions
- Works without VPN

**Limitations:**
- Limited English coverage in smaller cities and rural areas
- Indoor maps not available everywhere
- Search works better with Chinese characters

### 2. Amap / Gaode Maps (高德地图) — Most Accurate

Amap is China's leading navigation platform. It's the gold standard for driving, walking, and public transit directions.

**Strengths:**
- Most accurate road and POI data in China
- Real-time traffic with congestion prediction
- Excellent indoor navigation (malls, airports, train stations)
- Voice navigation in Chinese

**Language barrier:** The app is entirely in Chinese. Workaround: save your key destinations as Chinese text in your notes app, then copy-paste into Amap's search bar.

**How to install:** Available on iOS App Store and Android app stores. Search for "高德地图" or "Gaode Maps."

### 3. Baidu Maps (百度地图) — Best POI Database

Baidu Maps has the most comprehensive point-of-interest database in China, including small shops, restaurants, and local businesses that don't appear on other platforms.

**Strengths:**
- Largest POI database — find even tiny restaurants
- Street view imagery in many cities
- Excellent indoor navigation
- Integration with Baidu's AI for smart recommendations

**Limitations:** Same Chinese-language interface as Amap. Slightly less accurate driving directions.

### 4. DiDi (滴滴出行) — For Rides

When you need a car, [DiDi](/insights/didi-english-guide-china) replaces the need for map navigation entirely. You enter your destination in the app, and the driver handles the route.

**English support:** DiDi has an English interface accessible through the Alipay mini-program.

## Practical Tips for Navigation in China

### Before You Leave Home

1. **Save key addresses in Chinese.** Copy your hotel address, key restaurants, and attractions into a notes file. You can paste these into any Chinese map app.

2. **Download offline maps.** Both Apple Maps and Amap support offline map downloads. Get the city maps for your destinations.

3. **Screenshot your hotel card.** Take a photo of your hotel's business card (Chinese side) — you can show this to any taxi driver or paste the address into a map app.

4. **Learn your neighborhood name in Chinese.** Knowing how to say/write your area helps with any navigation scenario.

### While in China

1. **Use Apple Maps + Amap together.** Apple Maps for English interface, Amap when you need precise local data.

2. **Ask locals to type destinations.** If you can't find a place on the map, show your phone to a local and ask them to type or pin the location.

3. **Use WeChat location sharing.** When meeting someone, they can share their live location via WeChat, which you can navigate to in Apple Maps.

4. **Keep a backup battery.** Navigation apps drain battery fast, especially when using GPS continuously.

## Coordinate System Note (Technical)

China mandates the GCJ-02 coordinate system (a.k.a. "Mars coordinates"), which applies a deliberate offset to the standard WGS-84 GPS coordinates. Apps that don't account for this offset will show your position 100-600m away from the real location.

Apple Maps, Amap, and Baidu Maps all handle GCJ-02 correctly. Google Maps does not apply this correction for China, which is why your blue dot appears in the wrong place.
