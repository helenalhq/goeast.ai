## Restoration policy ｜ 恢复展示的条件

An English item may be (re)introduced only by ONE of:

1. **Source verification**: documented evidence of the text's author, edition,
   and a license permitting our use (public domain, or explicit permission).
   It is then displayed with author/version attribution.
2. **New authored summary**: written by us FROM the verified Chinese original
   (《周易》 core text, collated 2026-11-14), reviewed by a second editor, with
   the creation basis retained (source string + draft history). Displayed as
   **"现代英文概述 Modern English summary (GoEast)"** — never labeled as
   traditional text, a translation of record, or a direct quotation.

We do not assert authorization we cannot document, and we do not copy other
copyrighted translations.
