# Shanghai Arrival Check: pilot operations and release gates

## Product
First-time English-speaking visitors arriving at Shanghai Pudong with one city hotel booked. US$12.90 per itinerary, subject to checkout disclosures and applicable tax. The deliverable is an English email with a short plan, a PNG Chinese driver card, and an offline arrival packet PDF. No bookings, real-time support, live flight monitoring or promise that a payment method will work. If a hotel transfer already covers the need, recommend the free guide.

## Pilot service level
Accept payment only with complete trip details and a staffed primary and backup review window. The clock starts at the later of verified payment and complete details, using an immutable server timestamp. Target 2 hours and commit to at most 12 calendar hours; confirm an exact deadline with time zone before payment. Keep at least 12 hours between promised delivery and departure during the pilot; do not take payment if no slot fits. Quotes expire at the last eligible payment time; qualify late payment before accepting it, otherwise route to an authorized operator for a refund. First five orders are reviewed individually, at most two open paid orders at a time. Internal milestones: verify receipt within 15 minutes, draft within 60 minutes, review by 2 hours; if still waiting at 2 hours, notify the reviewer; escalate to backup no later than 6 hours or sooner if the slot is at risk. Never quietly reset the clock after a change. If the deadline cannot be met, notify and have an authorized operator process a full refund without limiting statutory rights. Do not advertise “usually within two hours” until timed end-to-end runs and actual observed service support the claim. Preview shows the targets as non-binding rehearsal values.

## Minimum live operation
1. Public form writes an order and version; ask only for customer email, scheduled PVG arrival date/time and airline/flight, exact hotel name (and address/official link if needed), and optional phone/payment constraint. No credentials, documents, booking reference, card information, hotel confirmation screenshots. Ask permission to use provided details for the service; no analytics of inputs.
2. Operator preflights scope, evidence availability and the review slot. Sends the customer the amount, deliverables, exact expiry/deadline and refund terms using an approved business payment provider (separate Creem product, subject to written approval for this description of the service; not the current I Ching Creem SKU). Confirm payment through provider, not email text, screenshot or redirect; match order/invoice ID, status, currency and amount; block refunds and disputes.
3. Operator marks payment verified, or a future verified provider webhook updates the order. In the same write, create a durable pending fulfillment row. GoEast notifies a scoped inbox or order label with opaque order ID only. Existing contact address and operational receiver are different; reuse only after a round-trip receipt, reply, and notification test. Receiving that mail triggers Emma only after the actual connected mailbox and sender filter are verified. Emma re-reads the order through narrowly scoped access before downloading any fields. A reviewer checks the pending queue at the start of each staffed window and promptly on reminders (flag missing acknowledgement at 15 minutes); email latency, retry, missing triggers, and duplicate events may not lose orders. Do not claim an email-trigger SLA before measuring it.
4. Emma prepares card and delivery email draft. An independent reviewer checks key facts and approves the exact version while payment/identity still match. Confirm recipient from order; no email overrides. On delivery store sent timestamp, version, and a mail message ID; on send-time ambiguity look up sent mail before retrying. Track bounce/manual recovery. For a missing fact store a follow-up draft; not a deliverable.
5. Delete unused trip information under the agreed retention schedule; keep accounting receipt information separately as applicable. No itinerary details in operational notifications or third-party analytics.

## Flight lookup: evidence and fallback

Shanghai Airport’s [public lookup](https://www.shairport.com/flights/index.html) covers yesterday, today and tomorrow, using Shanghai time. An out-of-range date is not an invalid flight. A source outage is a different status, as is no match inside the window. A dated, official operating schedule can support a **Scheduled** label and a pre-departure recheck reminder; it is not a live status result. A generic carrier terminal cannot verify the particular flight. For ambiguous codeshares, ask for “Operated by” first and consult the actual carrier (see [ANA guidance](https://www.ana.co.jp/fs/int/en/)); use the final inbound leg, its departure airport if needed and Shanghai-local arrival date/time. Never request screenshots or booking codes. Hold without payment if the destination or terminal still cannot be resolved. Regression: fictional Friday departure, Saturday Shanghai arrival outside the airport window, shared flight number, and two hotels with similar names. The local fixtures are illustrative only; no live feed is wired in.
