# Shanghai Arrival Check: pilot operations and release gates

## Product
First-time English-speaking visitors arriving at Shanghai Pudong with one city hotel booked. US$12.90 per itinerary, subject to checkout disclosures and applicable tax. The deliverable is an English email with a short plan, a PNG Chinese driver card, and a two-page offline PDF backup. No bookings, real-time support, live flight monitoring or promise that a payment method will work. If a hotel transfer already covers the need, recommend the free guide.

## Pilot service level
Show this only after named operations and backup reviewers are ready: “We confirm the delivery time before you pay. If we accept your request, we’ll email your plan within 48 hours of confirmed payment and complete trip details.” Display an exact deadline with time zone on the quote. Quote only with an assigned review slot (including weekends/holidays, otherwise pause sales). Require the agreed delivery deadline to be at least 24 hours before departure, normally request 72+ hours ahead. Close at two open paid orders initially. Decline before collecting if outside scope, disputed inputs or no reviewer. Flag internally at 24h; escalate to backup at 36h; if undeliverable, notify and offer full refund via an authorized operator without limiting statutory rights. Do not silently restart the clock on a change: agree on a replacement deadline or arrange a refund. Internal target 24h.

## Minimum live operation
1. Public form writes an order and version; ask only for customer email, scheduled PVG arrival date/time and airline/flight, exact hotel name (and address/official link if needed), and optional phone/payment constraint. No credentials, documents, booking reference, card information, hotel confirmation screenshots. Ask permission to use provided details for the service; no analytics of inputs.
2. Operator preflights scope, evidence availability and the review slot. Sends the customer the amount, deliverables, exact expiry/deadline and refund terms using an approved business payment provider (initially commercial invoices if permitted; not current I Ching Creem SKU). Confirm payment through provider, not email text, screenshot or redirect; match order/invoice ID, status, currency and amount; block refunds and disputes.
3. Operator marks payment verified, or a future verified provider webhook updates the order. In the same write, create a durable pending fulfillment row. GoEast notifies the dedicated inbox with opaque order ID only. Receiving that mail triggers Emma only after the actual connected mailbox and sender filter are verified. Emma re-reads the order through narrowly scoped access before downloading any fields. A reviewer checks the pending queue at least twice a day during pilot (plus deadline alerts); email latency, retry, missing triggers, and duplicate events may not lose orders. Do not claim an email-trigger SLA before measuring it.
4. Emma prepares card and delivery email draft. An independent reviewer checks key facts and approves the exact version while payment/identity still match. Confirm recipient from order; no email overrides. On delivery store sent timestamp, version, and a mail message ID; on send-time ambiguity look up sent mail before retrying. Track bounce/manual recovery. For a missing fact store a follow-up draft; not a deliverable.
5. Delete unused trip information under the agreed retention schedule; keep accounting receipt information separately as applicable. No itinerary details in operational notifications or third-party analytics.

## Fulfillment Skill contract (not installed in this branch)
Input: order ID and revision, payment verification separate from the event, confirmed travel fields, ticket cutoff and check time. Search airport/airline, transport operator and hotel public pages; never include email or a whole private itinerary in a public query. Mark each critical field as verified, customer-provided or unknown, with source URL, relevant excerpt/summary, scope (terminal/date), and observed time. Reconcile airline flight details vs. customer input; scheduled vs. actual are different. Official property page verifies name/address, not a specific booking. Check route and backup for this local time; a 24h desk is assistance, not a promise of a ride or accepted payment. Hold if terminal or property is ambiguous, evidence conflicts, or no supported feasible route. Avoid unsupported fares, timings, payment acceptance and hotel policies. Verify changed fields and invalidate old approval. Render email/PDF/PNG from same approved data; verify driver card shows no customer identity. Output delivery draft, attachments and private QA summary with unresolved items. No live send before reviewer authorization.

## Release acceptance before enabling real orders
- Verify commercial account eligibility and merchant details; a real paid order must use a separate product/invoice stream from I Ching. Verify test receipt→ pending row → inbox → draft → correct attachments → test inbox with replay protection.
- Test T1 vs T2, daytime vs after-midnight, ambiguous hotel, canceled/refunded order, changing data after approval, duplicate notice, lost email, deadline miss and bounced delivery. Check PDF/PNG offline, including readable Chinese.
- Assign primary and backup reviewers, confirm staffing and scoped connector; owner approves policy, final copy and opening sales. Pilot first five orders reviewed individually. Maintain a published pause control for intake and invoicing; do not leave paid orders without an owner.
